The Diagnostic Question:

“What script are you running that no longer serves your future?”

High performers rarely fail due to a lack of effort. They fail because they are unconsciously executing rules, defensive scripts, and expectations written for an earlier version of their life or organization. When forward is blocked, the first step is interrogating the operating story.

Core Interrogation:

“Is this decision serving who I am becoming, or who I used to be to survive?”
